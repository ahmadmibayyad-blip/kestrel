What it does while you are not looking

Automation rules
Combine any metrics and time windows. Pause, restart, raise or cut budgets, or just send an alert. Set a ceiling so a budget never runs away.
Preview before it runs
Every rule shows the campaigns it would change right now, with the numbers that triggered it. No surprises on Monday morning.
Bulk edits
Select twenty campaigns and change them in one move. Budgets, status, all from one table.
Creative analytics
See which ads earn their spend and which are wearing out, flagged when click rate starts to slide.
Reports
Break results down by platform, campaign or day. Export the table as a CSV in one click.
Change log
Every pause and budget change, with the rule or person that made it. Easy to audit, easy to undo.

From connected to automated in an afternoon

  1. Connect your ad accounts

    Link Meta, Google and TikTok. Kestrel reads your campaigns and the last 60 days of results.

  2. Write rules, or start from a template

    Stop losing campaigns, scale winners, catch tired creative. Adjust the numbers to match your margins.

  3. Let it run, then read the log

    Rules check as often as every 15 minutes. Each change is logged with the rule that made it.

Where Kestrel sits

Ad automation tools tend to take one of two approaches. Kestrel keeps the control of the first and removes most of the upkeep.

Rule builders

You decide exactly what happens and when. The cost is time: complex rule trees are slow to build and hard to read six months later.

AI optimizer suites

Fast to start, and they act on patterns you would miss. The cost is visibility: it can be hard to tell why a budget moved.

Kestrel

Rules written as sentences anyone on the team can read, a preview of what will change before it does, and a log of everything after.

Questions

Will a rule ever change something I did not expect?

Each rule shows the campaigns it matches before you turn it on, and budget rules take a ceiling or a floor. Everything a rule does is written to the change log.

How often do rules run?

You choose per rule, from every 15 minutes to once a day.

Is the demo using real data?

No. The demo account is sample data generated in your browser. Nothing in it touches a real ad account.
